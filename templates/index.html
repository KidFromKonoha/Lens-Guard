Viva & Presentation Summary

                
Plain-English Explainer for Examiners

              

            

            

              

                
1. Why Heuristic Regex Alone is Insufficient

                

                  Regex only detects literal keywords (e.g. ignore instructions). Attackers bypass this using roleplay or continuation framing. Upstream regex caught only 4 of 18 (22.2%) subtle injection prompts.
                

              


              

                
2. The Honest Trade-Off at T* = 0.72

                

                  At the zero-false-positive operating threshold (0.72), soft-worded prompts caught rise from 4 of 18 to 9 of 18, while preserving 0 of 30 false positives on benign documents (95% upper bound ≤ 10%).
                

              


              

                
3. Automated Near-Duplicate Check

                

                  Near-duplicate similarity check (≥ 80% Ratcliff/Obershelp ratio) found no overlap between training data and evaluation items. The system runs 100% locally and offline in ~6.4ms.
